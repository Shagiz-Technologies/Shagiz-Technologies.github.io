Independent application: TeleVault is developed by Shagiz Technologies and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram.

1. Scope

This policy explains how the official TeleVault Android application accesses, stores, transmits, protects, and deletes information. TeleVault uses the Telegram service through TDLib. Telegram's own privacy policy, terms, account controls, infrastructure, limits, and retention practices also apply.

A bucket is a private Telegram channel selected or created by the user for backup. TeleVault does not currently operate a separate Shagiz media-storage server.

2. Information TeleVault handles

Telegram authorization and account data

TeleVault processes the phone number, login code, optional Telegram two-step-verification password, Telegram profile information, channel information, messages, and file references needed to authorize and operate the user's Telegram account. Authentication requests are handled through TDLib and Telegram. TeleVault does not ask users to send credentials to Shagiz Technologies.

Photos, videos, and media metadata

With Android permission, TeleVault accesses photos and videos available to the application. It processes the media bytes and metadata needed for gallery display, organization, hashing, duplicate prevention, queueing, backup, restore, Vault processing, and diagnostics. Android 14 and later selected-media access is supported, so users may limit TeleVault to specific items.

Local operational data

TeleVault stores bucket records, labels, settings, queue state, upload operation identifiers, Telegram chat and message references, retry information, media access state, metadata-backup state, and local diagnostics in application-private storage. TDLib separately stores its account database, session, and downloaded files in application-private directories. Sensitive local values use platform secure storage where implemented.

3. Information sent to Telegram and other parties

Selected normal media, encrypted Vault objects, and encrypted metadata snapshots may be transmitted through TDLib to Telegram channels controlled through the signed-in Telegram account. Telegram processes account authorization, channels, messages, file transfer, storage, and retrieval.

A private Telegram channel is not the same as client-side end-to-end encryption. Telegram remains a third-party processor. Network providers, Android, Google Play, device vendors, browsers, and destinations selected through the Android share sheet may also process information relevant to their services.

The reviewed production application contains no advertising SDK and no external analytics SDK. Shagiz Technologies does not sell user media or Telegram account information.

4. Encryption and security boundaries

Normal backup limitation: Normal non-vault photos and videos are not client-side end-to-end encrypted by TeleVault before they are uploaded to Telegram.

Media intentionally added through the Vault flow is encrypted before its Vault upload using authenticated, chunked encryption. Each Vault object uses independent key material and a separate high-entropy Vault Recovery Key. A local PIN, password, or biometric unlock is a device convenience and is not a replacement for the Recovery Key.

Encrypted metadata v5 snapshots may be stored in Telegram for recovery. They exclude recovery secrets and portable copies exclude absolute device paths. Losing the Recovery Key may make Vault data and protected recovery material permanently unrecoverable.

No safeguard can guarantee security. Device compromise, weak device security, Telegram account compromise, software defects, dependency vulnerabilities, user error, and loss of recovery material remain possible.

5. Background backup

When Auto Backup is enabled, TeleVault may use an Android foreground data-sync service with a visible notification and WorkManager scheduling to resume queued work. Android, the device vendor, network conditions, battery restrictions, foreground-service limits, or user settings may delay, cancel, or stop background work.

Wi-Fi-only mode is enforced while uploads are active. If the required network is lost, TeleVault attempts to cancel or pause the transfer and returns unconfirmed work to a resumable state. Users should still verify important uploads directly in Telegram.

6. Android permissions

  • Photos and videos: display, organize, select, discover, back up, restore, and Vault supported media.
  • Internet: communicate with Telegram through TDLib and open user-selected web resources.
  • Notifications and foreground data sync: show and continue eligible backup work.
  • Biometric/device authentication: protect local app or Vault access when enabled.
  • Legacy external-storage read access: read media on older supported Android versions.

TeleVault does not request all-files access, media location access, contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, or call-log permissions in the reviewed release.

7. Retention and deletion

Local information remains until TeleVault removes it, the user logs out and completes account cleanup, Android app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Logout cancels scheduled background work and removes local account data, TDLib storage, temporary files, and account secrets. Depending on the option selected by the user, encrypted Vault objects and installed recovery material may be retained locally.

Logout, clearing app data, or uninstalling TeleVault does not automatically delete Telegram channels, messages, uploaded media, or metadata snapshots. Users must delete remote content through Telegram or supported TeleVault controls. Shagiz Technologies cannot directly access or delete content held in a user's Telegram account.

Detailed instructions are available on the Data & Deletion page.

8. Children and international processing

TeleVault is not designed or marketed specifically for children. Users must meet Telegram's eligibility requirements and applicable law. Telegram, Google, device vendors, and other service providers may process information in countries different from the user's country under their own policies.

9. Changes

This policy may change when TeleVault's features, dependencies, permissions, distribution, or legal obligations change. The date above will be updated when material changes are published.

10. Privacy inquiries

Shagiz Technologies provides a public inquiry mechanism through TeleVault GitHub Issues. Use it only for non-sensitive privacy or deletion-process questions.

Do not post phone numbers, Telegram identifiers, login codes, passwords, private media, databases, metadata packages, Recovery Keys, session files, or unredacted logs. Security vulnerabilities must be reported through GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting.
