Independent open-source Android app

Your media. Your Telegram space. Clear boundaries.

TeleVault organizes photos and videos and can back them up to private Telegram channels connected through your Telegram account.

View source code Read the privacy policy

No Shagiz media-storage backend

In the current implementation, Telegram and TDLib handle authentication, channel access, and uploaded files.

How it works

A local app connected to Telegram

Scan and organize

TeleVault reads selected device media and keeps file, bucket, label, and sync metadata in a local Drift/SQLite database.

Choose a bucket

A bucket maps to a private Telegram channel. TDLib uses your authenticated Telegram session to create or access it.

Upload with status

The local queue tracks pending, uploading, completed, and failed work. Availability still depends on the device, network, TDLib, and Telegram.

Security boundary

Vault encryption is not the same as normal backup

Normal uploads

Normal non-vault media is not currently client-side encrypted by TeleVault before it is uploaded to Telegram.

Vault-protected media

Media moved through the TeleVault vault flow is encrypted by TeleVault. Vault access can be protected with a TeleVault credential or supported device authentication.

Independent backups

Storage capacity, availability, and permanence are subject to Telegram's services and policies. Keep another backup of files you cannot replace.

Independent project: TeleVault is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram. Telegram and TDLib are third-party services and components with their own terms and privacy practices.
